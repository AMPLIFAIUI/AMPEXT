Automatic Capture
AMP runs quietly in the background, detecting when you're using supported AI platforms and capturing conversation data in real-time. No manual copying or exporting required.
Smart Memory Management
Conversations are stored in efficient memory pools with automatic deduplication. The extension uses minimal browser resources while maintaining quick access to your data.
Desktop Integration
Pair with the optional AMP desktop application to cascade your captured data to local SQLite storage. Keep your AI conversation history on your own machine with full offline access.
Data Injection
Retrieve and inject previously captured context back into your AI conversations. Reference past discussions without manually searching through chat histories.
Privacy Focused
All data stays on your device. AMP does not transmit your conversations to external servers. Your AI interactions remain private and under your control.

### Detailed Description

**🧠 AMP - Auto Memory Persistence**

**The Problem:**
Every AI chat has a memory limit. Long conversations get truncated. Close a tab? Context is gone. Switch devices? Start over. Your valuable AI conversations disappear into the void.

**The Solution:**
AMP automatically captures and preserves your AI conversations, giving you infinite context memory across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and 20+ AI platforms.

---

**✨ Key Features:**

🔄 **Automatic Capture**
AMP silently monitors your AI chats and saves every message in real-time. No copy-pasting, no manual exports—it just works.

💾 **Local Storage**
Your conversations stay on YOUR computer. No cloud uploads, no third-party servers. Your data, your control.

⬆️ **Context Injection**
When an AI "forgets" your conversation, AMP can inject previous context back into the chat, restoring continuity instantly.

🔗 **Cross-Session Memory**
Close your browser, restart your computer—your AI memory persists. Pick up exactly where you left off.

📊 **Memory Dashboard**
Visual interface showing your stored conversations, memory usage, and activity feed in real-time.

🔒 **Privacy First**
- All data stored locally on your PC
- No account required
- No data leaves your machine
- Open source and auditable

---

**🤖 Supported AI Platforms:**

• ChatGPT (chat.openai.com, chatgpt.com)

• Google Gemini

**💡 How It Works:**

1. **Install** - Add AMP to Chrome
2. **Chat** - Use your favorite AI as normal
3. **Automatic** - AMP captures conversations in the background
4. **Persist** - Data flows to local storage on your PC
5. **Restore** - Inject context when AI loses memory

### Privacy Policy Summary

AMP does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on external servers. All conversation data is stored exclusively on the user's local machine. The extension requires broad host permissions solely to detect and capture AI conversations across supported platforms. No analytics, tracking, or telemetry is performed.
