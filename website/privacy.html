Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2, 2025

Summary: AMP stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any user data. Your AI conversations remain private and under your control.

Introduction

AMP ("Automatic Memory Persistence") is a browser extension and optional desktop application developed by AMPiQ. This privacy policy explains how we handle user data.

Data Collection

AMP collects minimal data necessary to provide the service. The extension captures AI conversation content solely for local storage on your device.

What AMP captures locally:

  • Text content from AI chat conversations (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, etc.)
  • Timestamps of when conversations occurred
  • Metadata such as AI platform name and conversation topics
  • Your license key (stored locally for validation)

What AMP sends to our servers:

  • License key validation requests (to verify your subscription status)
  • No conversation content or personal data is transmitted

What AMP does NOT collect:

  • Browsing history outside of supported AI platforms
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Financial or payment information (handled by our payment processor)
  • Location data
  • Any data from websites other than supported AI chat platforms

Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Stripe, a trusted third-party payment processor. When you make a purchase:

  • Your payment information is collected and processed directly by Stripe
  • We receive your email address and license key information only
  • We do not receive, store, or have access to your credit card numbers, CVV, or full payment details
  • All payment data is handled securely by Stripe in compliance with PCI-DSS standards
  • Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data: stripe.com/privacy

Data Storage

All captured data is stored locally:

  • Browser Extension: Data is stored in Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device
  • Desktop Application: Data is stored in a local SQLite database file on your computer

No data is transmitted to external servers, cloud services, or third parties.

Data Transmission

AMP does not transmit any user data over the internet. The only network communication that occurs is:

  • Between the browser extension and the locally installed desktop application (via native messaging on localhost only)

This communication happens entirely on your local machine and does not involve any external servers.

Third-Party Services

AMP does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or similar tracking
  • Advertising networks
  • User behavior tracking
  • Crash reporting services that transmit data

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to any third parties. Since all data remains on your device, we have no access to it.

Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • Local-only storage (data never leaves your device)
  • Chrome's built-in extension security sandbox
  • Optional encryption for sensitive data in memory

User Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • View: Access all captured data through the extension popup or desktop app
  • Delete: Clear all stored data at any time through the extension settings
  • Export: Export your data for backup or transfer
  • Disable: Pause or disable capture at any time

Children's Privacy

AMP is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Your privacy is guaranteed: AMP is designed with privacy as a core principle. All data stays on your device, under your control, always.
