## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** Dark navy (#0a1628 or similar)
- **Accent Color 1:** Cyan/electric blue (#00d4ff or similar)
- **Accent Color 2:** Rainbow ring (gradient: cyan → magenta → orange → green)
- **Text Color:** Pure white (#ffffff)
- **Secondary accents:** Purple, pink, orange for neon effects

### Typography
- **Headline (Large):** Clean sans-serif, bold, 48-56pt
- **Body text:** Clean sans-serif, regular, 32-40pt
- **Small text:** 24-32pt
- **All text:** High contrast, anti-aliased, easily readable

### Animation Guidelines
- **Text appears:** Fade-in over 1-1.5 seconds
- **Text holds:** Minimum 4 seconds for reading
- **Text disappears:** Fade-out over 0.5-1 second
- **Transitions:** Cross-fade (1-1.5s) or fade-to-black (1s)
- **Camera moves:** Smooth, no jerky motions, 2-3s per movement
- **Logo animations:** Subtle glow pulse, rotating ring accent (slow, ~10s full rotation)

### Audio Levels
- **Ambient pad:** -18dB (subtle, underneath)
- **Tech tones:** -12dB (noticeable, not overwhelming)
- **Music melody:** -6dB (prominent but not dominating)
- **Overall mix:** Peaks at -3dB

### Music Specification
- **Genre:** Ambient electronic / synthwave
- **Tempo:** 80-90 BPM (slow, deliberate, premium feeling)
- **Key:** Minor or neutral (modern, tech aesthetic)
- **Instrumentation:** Synth pads, subtle synth melody, minimal drums/rhythm
- **Duration:** 60-65 seconds (allow fade-out at end)
- **Mood:** Innovative, trustworthy, powerful, forward-thinking

### Recommended Tools for Recreation
- **Video Editor:** Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, or Blender
- **Motion Graphics:** After Effects, Cinema 4D, Blender, or Motion
- **Audio:** Audition, Logic Pro, or dedicated audio editing software
